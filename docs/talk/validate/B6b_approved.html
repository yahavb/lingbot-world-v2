Two lanes, the same memory in both, running at the same time. The boxes are the model’s memory, the KV cache: what it saw in the three earlier chunks. Every new chunk has to look at all of it.

Top, copy first (the old way): before every attention call, all three chunks are copied into one new buffer and padded with zeros to the kernel’s size; then one call computes the answer. The red part of its time bar is the extra the copy costs. The zeros also get weight in the softmax, so the answer is further from fp32 (0.032). 7.37 s GEN per video.

Bottom, read in place (the fix): one call per chunk, each reading its chunk where it already is, into a small note (unnormalized output, row max, row sum). The notes are rescaled by how far each max is below the largest (×0.37, ×1, ×0.17) and added: exactly one softmax over all twelve keys. Error vs fp32 0.011, 4.34 s. It finishes while the top lane is still working.

Then two chunks per call: each call has a fixed start-up cost, so chunks 1 and 2 go into one call. 3 calls become 2, and 4.34 → 4.08 s.

Measured on 8 trn3 chips, TP4 × CP8, q20 flags with one flag off at a time (LINGBOT_CTE_MERGE, then LINGBOT_ATTN_PAIR): docs/talk/validate/RESULTS.md, Backups, B6a/B6b. The red segment is the measured difference 7.37 − 4.34 s, drawn to scale.
