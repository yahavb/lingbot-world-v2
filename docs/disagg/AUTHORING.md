# Instructions for a session that helps the authors write the paper

Give this file to the new session as its first message. It holds everything needed; nothing below needs this
session's history.

## The rule that comes first

OSDI '27 treats a paper "wholly or largely" generated by AI as fraud. The session helps the authors write: it
outlines, checks claims against data, makes tables and figures, and edits the authors' own text. It does not hand
over finished prose to submit. Coding-agent use is disclosed in the Implementation section.

## The claims and their evidence (all measured)

1. **Coupling, not either stage, limits co-located serving.**
   - Co-located: 15.5 / 22.6 / 26.7 fps at 4 / 8 / 16 chips (with async emit).
   - Split: 25.8 fps on 9 chips, 32.1 fps on 17 chips.
2. **The stages have different binding resources.**
   - DiT: on-chip data movement, with DMA busy 82% at 8.8x the HBM need. 37% of that DMA time is MXFP8 activation
     transposes, which are nkilib's input path. Three cheaper variants were measured, and all were slower.
   - VAE: array utilization, with the tensor engine busy about 90% at 44-50% of peak.
3. **The cost model** (co-located T_d + T_v, split max(T_d, T_v), k = T_d / T_v):
   - 15 cells within 1.4%;
   - held-out 16-chip DiT +2.5%;
   - streams per VAE predicted 3.7 and 2.1, against 3 and 2 measured.
4. **Rate matching works both ways.**
   - One VAE serves 3 streams with no slowdown.
   - A fast DiT needs more VAE: Stable Audio at 2 steps, 2+2 gives 1.22x while 2+1 loses; LingBot at 2 steps,
     4+2 gives 1.44x.
5. **Horizontal scaling per role.** 3 VAEs serve 9 streams at full speed (see hs3_9x3_* in summary.txt).
6. **Memory.**
   - 720p co-located 8 chips does not fit, while the split DiT does.
   - The per-session decoder cache is 427 MiB.
7. **Cost at real time.** 4.5 chips per stream split against 8 co-located: 44% fewer chips.
8. **Latency.** A 4-chip VAE matches the co-located median (1114 ms) and beats its p90 (1371 vs 1416 ms).
9. **The gain is the decoupling, not the pods.**
   - In one pod: 17.51 / 25.74 fps.
   - Across pods: 17.54 / 25.76 fps.
   - Placement on the same node vs the other node: no difference.
10. **Where it does not pay.**
    - LingBot at 8 chips: 22.6 co-located vs 17.5 split (4+x).
    - Stable Audio at 25-50 steps: 3-5%.
    - Elastic VAE cold start: 18 min, so it needs a warm standby.
